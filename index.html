How to choose a chat room

Start with your goal. If you want to make friends, look at open web chat and community platforms. If you want to practise a language, use a language-exchange service. For a group of your own, pick a team or community tool.

Then check for active moderation, clear rules and a size that suits you. Small topic rooms usually have better conversations than huge general rooms.

Chat room safety tips

Use a nickname, never share your address, school or workplace, and do not click unknown links. Report abuse to moderators and leave rooms that feel wrong. Children and teens should only use services designed for their age, with a parent aware.

Chat rooms FAQ

What are chat rooms?

Chat rooms are online spaces where people talk in real time, usually grouped by topic, interest or location.

Are online chat rooms safe?

They can be, if you choose moderated rooms, never share personal details, and leave any room that makes you uncomfortable. Children and teens should only use services built for their age.

How do I choose the right chat room?

Start with your goal, such as making friends, practising a language or joining a gaming group. Then look for active moderation, clear rules and a size that suits you.

Are these chat rooms free?

Most listed services offer a free way to join. Some have paid upgrades, so check each site's current terms.
